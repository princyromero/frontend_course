ABOUT SWIGGY

Swiggy is a popular online food delivery platform in India. It helps customers discover restaurants and order food online. Swiggy connects customers, restaurants and delivery partners through its mobile application and website.

Services Provided by Swiggy

  • Online Food Delivery
  • Restaurant Discovery
  • Online Food Ordering
  • Live Order Tracking
  • Quick Commerce
  • Restaurant Reviews and Information

How Swiggy Works

  1. Customer opens the Swiggy app or website.
  2. Customer searches for a restaurant or food.
  3. Customer selects the food and places the order.
  4. The restaurant prepares the food.
  5. A delivery partner collects the order.
  6. The delivery partner delivers the food to the customer.
