This is my funny story that happened during our second year of college with my friends.

Every year, our college conducts a symposium for the different departments. But in our college, all the departments don’t have their symposium on the same day. Each department has its own separate date and conducts its program on a different day. One day, it was the MBA department’s symposium. Normally, students from other departments were not allowed to attend their symposium. We were not supposed to go either. But that day, after the break, we decided to bunk our class and go to the MBA symposium anyway. We didn’t take permission from anyone, and we didn’t even participate in any of the events. We simply skipped class and went there.
We thought, “Okay, let’s stay for about an hour and then go back to our class. So after spending some time there, we decided to return. But while we were coming back, we got caught by the staff! They immediately started questioning us: “Who gave you permission to go there? You’re not even allowed to attend another department’s program. “Why did you go there? Did you participate in any of the events?” We had no proper answer. So they sent us directly to the HOD’s room. When we reached the HOD’s room, the HOD wasn’t there. He had gone to a meeting. We waited for some time, thinking he might come back. We waited and waited, but he still didn’t come.
Since it was almost time to leave college, we thought, “Okay, let’s just go home. We’ll deal with it tomorrow.” So we left. The next day, me and my friends went to the HOD’s room again. Finally, we got the chance to meet him. He gave us a good scolding and asked, “Who gave you permission to go to another department’s symposium? Why did you go there without permission?” We apologized and explained everything. Then he asked us to write a letter explaining what we had done. All of us wrote the letter and got the required signatures from the HOD, staff, and class in-charge. After everything was completed, finally the HOD  warned us: If you do something like this again, there will be serious action against you. We said, Okay sir and left. 
At that time, we were really scared. But now, when we remember it, it feels so funny.
It’s definitely one of those silly and funny memories from our second year that we still laugh about.
